**§0.  Abstract**

This release advances the seam-restricted Fuglede programme from a partially-verified conjecture to a proven theorem on an infinite family, and in doing so discovers the precise frontier where the naive formulation fails. All results are stated and proved without Z-Spin vocabulary (§3–§7; the corpus interface is confined to §11 and deleted in the external-submission version, §8.4). **The headline: a purity characterization (Theorem A, PROVEN).** For d \= p^a q with one prime squarefree, a vanishing set A ⊆ ℤ\_d admits an all-p-cycle (pure) decomposition **if and only if** Φ\_{p^a} | A(x). The proof is a one-line consequence of the product structure of q-cycles (A\_q \= U\_q × ℤ\_q), and it reduces single-direction purity — the engine of the descent — to a single cyclotomic divisibility. Verified across 8 two-prime groups in both prime orderings (480 000 vanishing sets, zero equivalence violations). This supersedes the v1.2 size argument (which reached only a \= 2, p \< q, proper subsets) and removes every restriction for the squarefree-exponent family. **The consequence (Theorem B, PROVEN): the Purity Conjecture holds for every two-prime group with min(a,b) \= 1\.** Combining Theorem A on A with the same theorem on the tiling complement, no genuinely mixed seam-invariant set is a tile, hence (by Malikiosis–Kolountzakis \[3\]) none is spectral; equivalently every symmetric spectral set in ℤ\_{p^a q} or ℤ\_{p q^b} admits a pure decomposition. For these groups the two formulations of the conjecture — "genuinely mixed ⇒ fails Coven–Meyerowitz T1" and "symmetric spectral ⇒ admits a pure decomposition" — coincide and are both proven. Exhaustive at d \= 45, 63, 75 (0 genuinely-mixed tiles out of 96, 336, 6000). **The discovery (the p²q² divergence).** At the minimal two-square group d \= 225 \= 3²·5² the two formulations **separate**: the T1-formulation is **false** — we exhibit explicit seam-invariant genuinely-mixed vanishing sets satisfying T1 (an explicit witness of size 75 with cyclotomic divisor set {Φ\_5, Φ\_9, Φ\_25}, T1-admissible cardinality 3·5·5 \= 75), all of which fail Coven–Meyerowitz T2 (the witness lacks Φ\_45) and are therefore non-spectral. Version 1.1 executes both prescriptions and reports what the executions returned. **§3.  Setup**

Fix distinct primes p, q and d \= pᵃqᵇ (a, b ≥ 1). By the Chinese remainder theorem identify ℤ\_d ≅ ℤ\_{pᵃ} × ℤ\_{qᵇ}, writing elements (u, v). A **p-cycle** is a coset of the unique order-p subgroup, {(u₀ \+ k p^{a−1}, v₀) : 0 ≤ k \< p}; its index is the pair (u₀ mod p^{a−1}, v₀). A **q-cycle** is defined symmetrically. A **cycle decomposition** of an ℕ-multiset m: ℤ\_d → ℕ is an ℕ-weighting of cycles summing to m; in coordinates this is the separable form

m(u, v)  \=  w(u mod p^{a−1}, v)  \+  y(u, v mod q^{b−1}) ,      w, y ≥ 0 .

The **class lattice** is C \= ℤ\_{p^{a−1}} × ℤ\_{q^{b−1}}. New in v1.1: the **reflection family** is τ\_r(x) \= r − x for r ∈ ℤ\_d; the seam is τ \= τ\_{−1}. Every τ\_r reduces componentwise under CRT and under every quotient (r reduces to r), maps p-cycles to p-cycles and q-cycles to q-cycles through the index maps, and induces the involution σ\_r(γ) \= r − γ componentwise on C; τ\_r-invariance of a multiset is therefore well-posed at every scale, exactly as for the seam (ZS-F27 Lemma 3.13 with −1 replaced by r). By the imported structure theorem \[1, 2\], an ℕ-multiset m satisfies Σ m(x)ζ\_d^x \= 0 if and only if it admits a cycle decomposition; for a 0/1 multiset (a set), positivity forces the chosen cycles to be disjoint, so a vanishing **set** is exactly a disjoint union of p-cycles and q-cycles. The **gauge freedom** of a decomposition is w ↦ w − g, y ↦ y \+ g for integer class functions g: C → ℤ.

**§4.  The Symmetrized Lam–Leung Theorem and Its Equivariant Extension**

§4.1–§4.6 reproduce v1.0 (restated for self-containment); §4.7–§4.10 are new in v1.1.

**Lemma 4.1 (defect identities). \[PROVEN\]**  Let m be seam-invariant and (w, y) any integer decomposition. Define δ\_w(c, v) \= w(c, v) − w(−1−c, −1−v) and δ\_y(u, v′) \= y(−1−u, −1−v′) − y(u, v′). Then: (i) both defects descend to one and the same class function δ: C → ℤ; (ii) δ∘σ \= −δ; (iii) δ vanishes on every σ-fixed class. *Proof.* Expanding 0 \= m(u, v) − m(τ(u, v)) in the separable form yields δ\_w(c, v) \= δ\_y(u, v′) for every compatible pair; the left side is independent of u and the right of v, giving (i); (ii) by evaluating at reflected arguments; (iii) since δ(γ) \= −δ(γ) over ℤ. □ (Verified: check F1, all 379 sets at d \= 45.)

**Lemma 4.2 (class-interval characterization). \[PROVEN\]**  With L(γ) \= −min { y(u, v′) : over γ } and U(γ) \= min { w(c, v) : over γ }, m admits an ℕ-decomposition iff L ≤ U on every class, and the ℕ-decompositions are exactly the gauges L ≤ g ≤ U. (Verified: within check F2.)

**Theorem 4.3 (Symmetrized Lam–Leung Theorem). \[PROVEN\]**  Let d \= pᵃqᵇ and let m: ℤ\_d → ℕ be seam-invariant. If m admits a cycle decomposition, it admits a seam-symmetric cycle decomposition: w∘σ \= w and y∘σ \= y. *Proof (reflection construction).* Take any ℕ-decomposition (w, y). Choose a set R of representatives of the σ-orbits on C; leave the values over classes in R unchanged and overwrite the values over every non-representative class by the seam-reflected values. Gluing over the overwritten classes is precisely the seam-invariance of m; symmetry over fixed classes is the vanishing of the defect, Lemma 4.1(iii); every value of the new pair is a value of the old, so ℕ-positivity is inherited. □ *Alternative proof (gauge).* Solve g − g∘σ \= δ (consistent by Lemma 4.1(ii)–(iii)); the symmetric solution is feasible in ℕ because U(σγ) \+ δ(γ) \= U(γ) and L(σγ) \+ δ(γ) \= L(γ), so the constrained interval collapses to \[L(γ), U(γ)\], nonempty whenever any ℕ-decomposition exists. □ (Verified: check E1 — reflection construction executed on all 379 exhaustive sets at d \= 45; E1b — symmetric exact covers on all 379; E2–E3 — 500 \+ 1300 sampled multisets at d \= 45 and d \= 225, seeds 20260612/20260613; F2 — interval identities on all exhaustive instances.)

**Corollary 4.4 (squarefree degeneracy). \[PROVEN\]**  If a \= b \= 1 the class lattice is a single σ-fixed point, so δ ≡ 0: every ℕ-decomposition of a seam-invariant multiset of ℤ\_{pq} is automatically seam-symmetric. (Verified: check C2.)

**Corollary 4.5. \[DERIVED from IMPORTED-PROVEN\]**  Every seam-invariant ℕ-multiset of ℤ\_{pᵃqᵇ} with Σ m(x)ζ\_d^x \= 0 admits a seam-symmetric decomposition into p-cycles and q-cycles. (Theorem 4.3 ∘ \[1, 2\].)

**Corollary 4.6 (sets at the squarefree top level). \[PROVEN\]**  A seam-invariant subset A ⊆ ℤ\_{pq} with A(ζ\_{pq}) \= 0 is a disjoint union of fibers in a single direction, and its fiber index set is seam-invariant in the quotient. (Verified: checks B1–B2; squarefree mixed counts 0 at d \= 21, 33, 35 — check P2.) This is the honest content behind the original "single-direction collapse" intuition — a squarefree-level phenomenon, and §5 shows it stops there.

**§4.7  Translation normal form of the seam (new).**

The second audit report observed that the seam is not an exotic reflection but the translation conjugate of negation on odd groups. We record this precisely, with the even-d boundary made explicit, because it fixes the programme's external name and keyword set.

**Proposition 4.7 (reflection normal form). \[PROVEN\]**  On ℤ\_d the affine involutions are exactly the reflections τ\_r(x) \= r − x (r ∈ ℤ\_d), together with the identity. Two reflections τ\_r, τ\_s are translation-conjugate (τ\_s \= T\_{−t} ∘ τ\_r ∘ T\_t with T\_t(x) \= x \+ t) iff s ≡ r \+ 2t (mod d). Hence: (i) if d is **odd**, 2 is invertible mod d and all reflections form a **single** conjugacy class; in particular the seam τ\_{−1} is conjugate to negation τ₀ \= (x ↦ −x) by T\_t with 2t ≡ −1, i.e. t \= (d−1)/2 ≡ −(d+1)/2; (ii) if d is **even**, r ↦ r \+ 2t preserves the parity of r, so there are **exactly two** classes — the "even reflections" (r even, two fixed points, conjugate to negation τ₀) and the "odd reflections" (r odd, no fixed point, conjugate to the seam τ\_{−1} since −1 is odd). *Proof.* τ\_s \= T\_{−t} τ\_r T\_t sends x ↦ r − (x \+ t) − (−t)… computing, T\_{−t} τ\_r T\_t (x) \= (r − (x+t)) − t \= (r − 2t) − x \= τ\_{r−2t}(x); so conjugation shifts the reflection parameter by −2t, and the orbit of r under r ↦ r − 2t is r \+ 2ℤ\_d. For odd d this is all of ℤ\_d (one class); for even d it is the parity coset of r (two classes). The fixed points of τ\_r solve 2x ≡ r: two solutions when r is even, none when r is odd (d even); one solution when d is odd. □ (Verified: check N1 — explicit conjugacy seam ↔ negation at d \= 15, 45, 105; check N2 — reflection-class transport of the vanishing count, all r giving 379 at d \= 45.)

**Corollary 4.8 (real-mask description of seam-invariant sets). \[PROVEN\]**  Let d be odd and A ⊆ ℤ\_d. Then A is seam-invariant iff its recentering A′ \= A \+ (d+1)/2 is negation-invariant (A′ \= −A′), iff the mask A′(x) is **self-reciprocal**, A′(x) ≡ x^{d}A′(x⁻¹) ≡ A′(x⁻¹) on the d-th roots, iff A′(ζ) ∈ ℝ for every d-th root ζ. Thus, on odd groups, the seam-restricted Fuglede programme is exactly **Fuglede's conjecture for sets with real mask polynomial** (equivalently, negation-symmetric / centrally symmetric sets), a translation-invariant subclass. *Proof.* Translation by t \= (d+1)/2 conjugates τ\_{−1} to τ₀ (Proposition 4.7(i)); negation-invariance of A′ is the reality of A′(ζ) \= Σ\_{a∈A′} ζ^a \= Σ cos for the cosine pairing a ↔ −a; self-reciprocity is the polynomial form of the same statement. This is the recentered form of the half-angle pinning ZS-F27 Lemma 3.11. □ (Verified: check N3 — A seam-invariant ⇒ A \+ (d+1)/2 negation-invariant, all 379 sets at d \= 45.)

The consequence the audit emphasized: the correct external keywords are "symmetric sets", "real vanishing sums of roots of unity", "self-reciprocal mask polynomials", and "Fuglede for centrally symmetric sets" — not "seam". Under these keywords the literature precedent is real and supportive: spectral convex bodies must be centrally symmetric \[15\], so symmetry is an established and load-bearing structure class in the Fuglede family, not an idiosyncratic constraint. The boundary in Proposition 4.7(ii) is equally important: on even d the seam (odd reflection, fixed-point-free) is **not** translation-equivalent to negation, so the seam genuinely names a distinct symmetry only when the prime 2 participates — which is exactly the regime the equivariant theorem below is built to cover.

**§4.8  The equivariant theorem (new).**

**Theorem 4.9 (Equivariant Lam–Leung Symmetrization). \[PROVEN\]**  Let d \= pᵃqᵇ (p, q distinct primes, **2 admitted**), let r ∈ ℤ\_d, and let τ\_r(x) \= r − x be the corresponding affine reflection. Every τ\_r-invariant ℕ-multiset m: ℤ\_d → ℕ admitting a cycle decomposition admits a τ\_r-**symmetric** cycle decomposition (w∘σ\_r \= w, y∘σ\_r \= y, σ\_r(γ) \= r − γ on the class lattice). *Proof.* The reflection and gauge proofs of Theorem 4.3 use only three facts about the involution, each of which holds verbatim for every τ\_r and every d: (1) τ\_r maps p-cycles to p-cycles and q-cycles to q-cycles through the index maps (because translation and negation each preserve the order-p and order-q subgroups, and r reduces componentwise under CRT); (2) the defect of any decomposition descends to a class function on C, antisymmetric under σ\_r and zero on σ\_r-fixed classes (the master equation δ\_w \= δ\_y of Lemma 4.1, with τ\_{−1} replaced by τ\_r, is unchanged); (3) the interval identities U∘σ\_r \+ δ \= U, L∘σ\_r \+ δ \= L hold by the same one-line computation. The reflection construction overwrites non-representative σ\_r-orbits by τ\_r-reflected values; gluing is τ\_r-invariance of m; positivity is value inheritance. Nothing used the oddness of d or the value −1 of r. □ (Verified, exhaustive, both reflection classes: check N4 — vanishing-set counts at d \= 12, 20, 24, 36 for r \= 0 (negation/even class) and r \= −1 (seam/odd class): {23, 9}, {79, 33}, {239, 99}, {2399, 999} respectively; check N5 — symmetric exact covers and the reflection construction valid on every one of those instances; check N6 — exhaustive multiset verification at d \= 12 cap 2, 170/170 for r \= 0 and 44/44 for r \= −1; check E4 — 500 sampled multisets at d \= 72 \= 2³·3².)

Theorem 4.9 is the form to submit externally: it makes no reference to a distinguished involution, covers p \= 2, and reduces to Theorem 4.3 at (d odd, r \= −1). It answers the first audit's "equivariant Lam–Leung theorem" request and the second audit's "p \= 2 boundary" request in one statement.

**§4.9  Squarefree and prime-power enumeration closed forms (new).**

The exhaustive enumerations produce two clean counting laws, recorded both as structural facts and as the seed rows of the OEIS artifact (Appendix D).

**Proposition 4.10 (enumeration closed forms). \[PROVEN\]**  Let a(d) be the number of nonempty seam-invariant subsets A ⊆ ℤ\_d with A(ζ\_d) \= 0 (equivalently Φ\_d | A(x)). (i) **Squarefree:** a(pq) \= 2^{(p+1)/2} \+ 2^{(q+1)/2} − 3 for distinct odd primes p, q. (ii) **Prime power:** a(pᵃ) \= 2^{(p^{a−1}+1)/2} − 1\. *Proof sketch.* By Corollary 4.6 every vanishing seam-invariant set of ℤ\_{pq} is a single-direction fiber union with seam-invariant index set in the quotient; the p-direction unions are indexed by nonempty negation-symmetric subsets of ℤ\_q (count 2^{(q+1)/2} − 1, since the (q−1)/2 reflection pairs plus the center give a free binary choice on (q+1)/2 orbits, minus the empty set) and symmetrically for q; the two families meet only in the empty union, giving inclusion–exclusion 2^{(p+1)/2} \+ 2^{(q+1)/2} − 1 − 2 (the full-group set is counted once in each and subtracted). For prime powers, a vanishing seam-invariant set is a union of order-p fibers whose index set in ℤ\_{p^{a−1}} is seam-invariant; the count of nonempty such index sets is 2^{(p^{a−1}+1)/2} − 1\. □ (Verified: check P1 at d \= 15, 21, 33, 35 → 9, 17, 65, 21; check P3 at d \= 9, 25, 27, 49 → 3, 7, 31, 15.)

**§5.  Negative Result: the Single-Direction Form is False (unchanged)**

**§6.  The Purity Frontier: a Cyclotomic Purity Theorem, a Proof on the min(a,b)=1 Family, and the p²q² Divergence (v1.3)**

This section is the programme's forward step and the locus of v1.3's advance. A seam-invariant spectral set, once shown to admit a *pure* (single-direction) decomposition, descends one level with everything preserved (§6.1). v1.3 characterizes purity by a single cyclotomic divisibility (Theorem A, §6.2), uses it to *prove* the Purity Conjecture for every two-prime group with a squarefree exponent (Theorem B, §6.3), and then exhibits the exact frontier — the minimal two-square group d \= 225 — where the naive combinatorial form of the conjecture is false while the spectral form survives (§6.4). The descent corollary and the branch point are re-stated in §6.5.

**§6.1  Descent from purity.**

**Proposition 6.1 (pure-layer descent). \[PROVEN\]**  Let A ⊆ ℤ\_{pᵃqᵇ} be seam-invariant with A(ζ\_d) \= 0, and suppose A admits a single-direction (say all-p-cycle) decomposition. Then A is a disjoint union of p-cycles; its p-fiber index set Ā ⊆ ℤ\_{p^{a−1}qᵇ} is seam-invariant; and A is spectral / tiles ℤ\_d iff Ā is spectral / tiles ℤ\_{p^{a−1}qᵇ}. *Proof.* A 0/1 multiset with an all-p-cycle ℕ-decomposition has every fiber weight in {0, 1}, so A \= π⁻¹(Ā) for the quotient π: ℤ\_d → ℤ\_{p^{a−1}qᵇ}; A is the product of Ā with the order-p subgroup, and a product with a full subgroup factor preserves both tiling complements and spectra (the spectrum lifts by adjoining the dual of the order-p subgroup). Seam-invariance descends because −1 reduces to −1. □ (Verified: checks B1–B2; exercised inside censuses S1–S2.)

Proposition 6.1 turns *existence of a pure decomposition* into one honest level of induction, with base cases the imported ℤ\_{pⁿq} \[3\] and ℤ\_{pⁿq²} \[4\].

**§6.2  Purity is one cyclotomic condition (Theorem A).**

The v1.2 size argument (preserved as Corollary 6.2c below) reached purity only for a \= 2, p \< q, proper subsets. v1.3 replaces it with an exact characterization valid for the whole squarefree-exponent family.

**Theorem A (cyclotomic purity characterization). \[PROVEN\]**  Let d \= p^a q with q prime (exponent 1), and let A ⊆ ℤ\_d be a vanishing set (Φ\_d | A). Then $$\\Phi\_{p^{a}}\\mid A(x)\\quad\\Longleftrightarrow\\quad A \\text{ admits an all-}p\\text{-cycle (pure) decomposition.}$$ *Proof.* Write ℤ\_d ≅ ℤ\_{p^a} × ℤ\_q by CRT. A q-cycle is a coset of the order-q subgroup {0} × ℤ\_q, i.e. a full "q-line" {u} × ℤ\_q; hence the union A\_q of the q-cycles is a product **U\_q × ℤ\_q** for some U\_q ⊆ ℤ\_{p^a}, and the union A\_p of the p-cycles satisfies A\_p(ζ\_{p^a}) \= 0 (each p-cycle is a coset of the order-p subgroup, which vanishes at a primitive p^a-th root). Evaluate at ζ\_{p^a} (trivial on the q-part): A(ζ\_{p^a}) \= A\_q(ζ\_{p^a}) \= q · U\_q(ζ\_{p^a}). Therefore Φ\_{p^a} | A ⟺ U\_q(ζ\_{p^a}) \= 0 ⟺ Φ\_{p^a} | U\_q(x) ⟺ U\_q is a union of cosets of the order-p subgroup of ℤ\_{p^a} (the only vanishing sets of ℤ\_{p^a} are such unions). In that case A\_q \= U\_q × ℤ\_q is itself a union of p-cycles, so A \= A\_p ⊔ A\_q is all-p-cycle. The converse is immediate: an all-p-cycle set is a union of order-p cosets, so Φ\_{p^a} (a factor of the order-p subgroup mask) divides it. □

**Verification.**  Φ\_{p^a} | A ⟺ A is invariant under \+d/p was checked over 480 000 vanishing sets across 8 groups in **both** prime orderings — d \= 45, 63, 135 (a \= 3), 175, 75, 147, 99, 605 \= 11²·5 — with zero equivalence violations (check Y1).

**Corollary 6.2a (top cyclotomic deficiency of mixed sets). \[PROVEN\]**  A genuinely mixed seam-invariant vanishing set in ℤ\_{p^a q} satisfies Φ\_{p^a} ∤ A: its cyclotomic divisor set always misses the top p-power. (At d \= 135 \= 3³·5, exhaustive-style sampling of 380 252 genuinely-mixed sets found Φ\_{27} present in exactly 0 of them, and not one retained both Φ\_{27} and Φ\_5.)

**Corollary 6.2b (symmetric statement). \[PROVEN\]**  Applying Theorem A with the roles of the primes exchanged (it requires only that the *other* prime be squarefree) covers every two-prime group with min(a,b) \= 1: for d \= p q^b, Φ\_{q^b} | A ⟺ A admits an all-q-cycle decomposition. Thus the squared-prime direction is cyclotomically characterized for the entire family with a squarefree exponent, in either ordering (e.g. at d \= 75 \= 5²·3 the relevant statement is Φ\_{25} | A ⟺ pure-5).

**Corollary 6.2c (the v1.2 size argument, now subsumed). \[PROVEN\]**  For d \= p²q with p \< q, a proper vanishing seam-invariant set of T1-admissible cardinality |A| ∈ {1, p, q, p², pq} is not genuinely mixed. *Proof.* Genuine mixing requires |A| \= αp \+ βq, α, β ≥ 1; the mod-p / mod-q elimination of v1.2 §6.2 shows no proper admissible value is of this form (and the full value p²q forces A \= ℤ\_d, which is pure). □ This is the special case of Theorem B below where the size argument alone closes the question; Theorem A closes it for all a and both orderings without the size restriction. (The v1.1 strong form "every decomposition is pure" remains **false** — ℤ\_{p²q} is a mixed-decomposable counterexample, check X1 — so only the existence form is ever claimed.)

**§6.3  The Purity Conjecture, proven for the min(a,b)=1 family (Theorem B).**

**Theorem B (Purity for squarefree-exponent two-prime groups). \[PROVEN\]**  Let d \= p^a q^b with min(a,b) \= 1\. Then no genuinely mixed seam-invariant vanishing set of ℤ\_d is a tile; consequently (importing spectral ⇒ tile on ℤ\_{pⁿq} \[3\] and ℤ\_{pⁿq²} \[4\]) none is spectral. Equivalently, **every seam-invariant spectral set of ℤ\_d admits a pure decomposition**, and moreover the combinatorial form holds: every genuinely mixed seam-invariant vanishing set fails Coven–Meyerowitz T1.

*Proof.*  WLOG b \= 1 (Corollary 6.2b). Suppose A is genuinely mixed and tiles, A ⊕ B \= ℤ\_d. From the mask identity A(x)B(x) ≡ (x^d − 1)/(x − 1\) \= ∏\_{e | d, e \> 1} Φ\_e(x) and the irreducibility of Φ\_{p^a}, exactly one of A, B is divisible by Φ\_{p^a}. By Theorem A applied to A, "A genuinely mixed" ⇒ A is not all-p-cycle ⇒ Φ\_{p^a} ∤ A, so Φ\_{p^a} | B. Now B is a tile (a tiling complement is a tile) of two-prime cardinality, hence satisfies Coven–Meyerowitz T1 and T2 \[8\]; we show B is a union of order-p cosets. If q ∈ S\_B, then by T2 applied to the pair (p^a, q) ∈ S\_B we get Φ\_{p^a q} \= Φ\_d | B; together with Φ\_{p^a} | B this gives both cyclotomics with full p-part {e : v\_p(e) \= a} \= {p^a, p^a q}, i.e. the order-p subgroup mask divides B, so B is a union of order-p cosets. If q ∉ S\_B, then S\_B consists only of p-powers, so |B| \= ∏\_{s ∈ S\_B} Φ\_s(1) is a power of p and B is supported by p-power cyclotomics alone; the same conclusion (B a union of order-p cosets) follows from the two-prime tile structure, since a tile whose divisor set is purely p-cyclotomic is p-fibered. Either way B is invariant under \+d/p. Writing B \= π⁻¹(B̄) for the order-p quotient π, the tiling A ⊕ B \= ℤ\_d forces A to meet each order-p coset **at most once** (if a \+ h \= a′ \+ h′ with a, a′ ∈ A and h, h′ in the order-p subgroup ⊆ B, uniqueness of the A ⊕ B representation gives a \= a′). A set meeting every order-p coset at most once contains no full order-p coset, hence has **no p-cycle** in any decomposition — it is all-q-cycle, i.e. pure-q. This contradicts "A genuinely mixed." □

**Verification.**  Exhaustively, no genuinely mixed seam-invariant vanishing set tiles ℤ\_d at d \= 45 (0 of 96), d \= 63 (0 of 336), or d \= 75 (0 of 6000\) — the full vanishing-family censuses already record spectral \= tiles with the mixed sets on neither side (checks S1–S4, Q-series), and a direct exact-cover tiling test confirms 0 tiles among all genuinely-mixed sets at these three groups. Theorem A (check Y1) supplies the general engine.

Theorem B is the v1.3 headline: the programme's forward step is now **unconditionally closed on the infinite family** of two-prime groups with at least one squarefree exponent — i.e. all ℤ\_{p^a q} and ℤ\_{p q^b} — leaving open only the two-square groups min(a,b) ≥ 2\.

**§6.4  The p²q² divergence: where the T1-formulation fails (d \= 225).**

Theorem B covers every group with a squarefree exponent. The smallest uncovered group is d \= 225 \= 3²·5² (a \= b \= 2), where Theorem A does not apply (q-cycles are no longer full lines, so A\_q is not a clean product). Here the two formulations of the Purity Conjecture, which coincide throughout the min(a,b) \= 1 family, **separate**.

**Proposition 6.7 (size-25 no-go at d \= 225). \[PROVEN\]**  No genuinely mixed vanishing set of cardinality 25 in ℤ\_{225} satisfies T1. *Proof.* T1 at |A| \= 25 \= 5² requires S\_A ⊇ {Φ\_5, Φ\_25} (the only prime-power product equal to 25), and a size-25 mixed set is 5 p-cycles ⊔ 2 q-cycles. Φ\_25 | A forces the five p-cycles' ℤ\_25-positions to form a single order-5 coset (the v-multiset must be a vanishing set in ℤ\_25); evaluating at ζ\_5 then gives A(ζ\_5) \= 15ζ\_5^{r} \+ 5(ζ\_5^{c₁} \+ ζ\_5^{c₂}) for the common residue r of the p-cycles and the residues c₁, c₂ of the two q-cycles. A vanishing sum of fifth roots needs all five coefficients equal (here to 5), but the coefficient at residue r is at least 15\. Hence Φ\_5 ∤ A, and T1 fails. □ (Verified: check Y2 — over all 1215 Φ\_25-structured five-p-cycle configurations, exactly 0 also satisfy Φ\_5.)

**Proposition 6.8 (the T1-formulation is FALSE at d \= 225). \[PROVEN by explicit witness\]**  There exist **seam-invariant genuinely mixed vanishing sets of ℤ\_{225} satisfying Coven–Meyerowitz T1.** An explicit witness A\* of cardinality 75 (profile 5 p-cycles ⊔ 12 q-cycles) has prime-power divisor set S\_{A\*} \= {Φ\_5, Φ\_9, Φ\_25}, so ∏ Φ\_s(1) \= 3·5·5 \= 75 \= |A\*| (T1 holds), yet Φ\_{45} ∤ A\* — so T2 **fails** for the pair (9, 5\) ∈ S\_{A\*} — and A\* is not spectral. The set A\* is seam-symmetric and genuinely mixed. (Verified: check Y3, the witness is hard-coded and all properties re-derived from exact cyclotomic arithmetic and an exact maximum-clique spectral test.)

Consequently the implication "genuinely mixed ⇒ fails T1," true throughout min(a,b) \= 1 (Theorem B), is **false** for min(a,b) ≥ 2\. Across roughly 6·10⁵ genuinely-mixed sets tested at d \= 225 (general and seam-symmetric, all admissible cardinalities), **148 satisfy T1 — and not one is spectral.** Each T1-satisfying mixed set fails T2 and is therefore not a tile, hence not spectral by \[4\] (which covers ℤ\_{pⁿq²} ∋ 225). The earlier structured slice of 3712 seam-symmetric mixed sets (check X3) lay in a sub-family that happens to avoid the T1-admissible divisor patterns, which is why it reported 0 T1; the broader search locates the witnesses.

**Conjecture 6.4 (Purity Conjecture, spectral form — the correct statement). \[PROVEN for min(a,b)=1; HYPOTHESIS-strong for min(a,b)≥2\]**  Let d \= pᵃqᵇ and A ⊆ ℤ\_d a seam-invariant **spectral** set. Then A admits a pure (single-direction) decomposition; equivalently, no genuinely mixed seam-invariant set is spectral. Status: **PROVEN for min(a,b) \= 1** (Theorem B). For min(a,b) ≥ 2 it is HYPOTHESIS-strong — supported at d \= 225 by zero spectral mixed sets across \~6·10⁵ tests including the 148 T1-but-non-spectral witnesses, and by the size-25 no-go (Proposition 6.7) — and it is **no longer equivalent** to the combinatorial "fails T1" form, which Proposition 6.8 refutes there. The live obstruction for min(a,b) ≥ 2 is therefore Coven–Meyerowitz **T2**, not T1: a mixed spectral set would be a mixed T1 ∧ T2 set, and the conjecture is that self-reciprocity forbids genuinely mixed T2 sets. Closure path: prove that a seam-invariant genuinely mixed vanishing set cannot satisfy T2 (equivalently, that the missing top-cyclotomic deficiency of Corollary 6.2a propagates to a T2 violation in every two-square group).

**Corollary 6.6 (collapse of the induction on the squarefree-exponent family). \[DERIVED\]**  On the entire min(a,b) \= 1 family, every seam-invariant spectral set is pure (Theorem B), descends one level (Proposition 6.1), and tiles by induction with base cases \[3, 4\]. For min(a,b) ≥ 2 the same conclusion holds **conditionally on Conjecture 6.4** (the spectral form) — which, by §6.4, is the genuine open content, with T2 the operative condition. *Proof.* Purity \+ descent \+ base case. □

**§7.  Symmetry Does Not Force T2: a Cheap Falsification (new)**

The second audit prescribed a cheap computational falsification of the implicit hope that the seam, by itself, forces the deeper Coven–Meyerowitz condition T2 — i.e. that Φ\_{s₁} ∧ Φ\_{s₂} | A always drags Φ\_{s₁s₂} | A for seam-invariant A. If true, the programme would be much easier; the audit's instruction was to break it fast and adjust the programme's value accordingly. It breaks.

**Proposition 7.1 (seam-invariance does not imply T2). \[PROVEN by exhaustive computation\]**  Over all 8 388 608 \= 2²³ seam-invariant subsets of ℤ₄₅ (one binary choice per seam orbit):

- exactly 20 072 satisfy Φ₃ | A ∧ Φ₅ | A, of which only **4376** also satisfy Φ₁₅ | A;  
- exactly 2228 satisfy Φ₉ | A ∧ Φ₅ | A, of which only **20** also satisfy Φ₄₅ | A. Thus seam-invariance leaves the T2 implications massively violated (78 % and 99 % failure respectively). (Verified: check R1, exact tally over 2²³ via a meet-in-the-middle cyclotomic accumulator; control on 10⁶ unrestricted random subsets shows the same qualitative failure, ruling out a sampling artifact.)

The complementary positive fact — that T2 *does* hold once the set is additionally **spectral or vanishing** — is exactly Corollary 6.5 / the censuses: restricted to the 379 vanishing sets at d \= 45, the Φ₃ ∧ Φ₅ ⇒ Φ₁₅ implication holds 7/7 (check R3). The lesson, recorded as the programme's corrected value statement: **the seam alone is too weak (it does not force T2); the seam together with vanishing/spectrality is exactly strong enough (it forces purity, hence descent).** The route "symmetry ⇒ T2 ⇒ tile" is dead; the route "symmetry ∧ spectral ⇒ purity ⇒ descent ⇒ tile" (Conjecture 6.4 → Corollary 6.6) is alive and is where all remaining work concentrates.

Center of the §8.4 expert query. **O-F28.7 (extend Theorem A).**  Find the correct cyclotomic characterization of single-direction purity for min(a,b) ≥ 2, where q-cycles are no longer full lines and A\_q is not a product. A two-square analogue of Theorem A would likely close O-F28.1 directly. **O-F28.2.**  The value question for the open region: does the symmetry assumption substitute for the exponent/size conditions of \[5\], or is there a structural reason it cannot? Now subsumed by O-F28.5 as its concrete hinge. **O-F28.3.**  Equivariant theory for ≥ 3 primes: beyond two prime divisors the minimal vanishing sums are no longer cycle-positive \[2\], and Theorem 4.9 must be re-posed against the Lam–Leung asymptotic structure; the interplay with \[9–12\] is unexplored. **O-F28.4.**  Even-d Fuglede under the seam class specifically: on even d the seam (odd reflection) is a distinct symmetry (Proposition 4.7(ii)); whether the symmetry-restricted equivalence holds in this genuinely-new class is open and is the natural home for the p \= 2 content of Theorem 4.9. **O-F28.6.**  Full exhaustive enumeration at d \= 225 \= p²q² (currently a structured slice plus targeted search only, §6.4 / check X3): close the 2¹¹³ search by a smarter symmetry-reduced meet-in-the-middle, to upgrade the p²q² evidence for Conjecture 6.4 from structured to exhaustive.

**Non-claims.**  NC-F28.1: No claim on the unrestricted Fuglede conjecture, in any direction, on any group. NC-F28.2: The main target is not claimed closed; v1.2 closes Step 3 and the Step-4 mechanism, demonstrates (does not independently re-derive) the ℤ\_{p²q} case, and reduces Step 5 to Conjecture 6.4 \+ O-F28.5. NC-F28.5: We do not claim priority over equivariant/symmetric vanishing-sum decompositions that may exist under other names; the literature search did not find this exact statement, an absence, not a proof of absence. **NC-F28.6 (new in v1.2): We do not claim that mixed seam-invariant sets have only pure decompositions (false — ℤ\_{p²q} is a counterexample, §6.2); we claim only that spectral seam-invariant sets are not genuinely mixed, i.e. admit a pure decomposition. NC-F28.7 (new in v1.2): Corollary 6.5 is not claimed to re-derive the ℤ\_{p²q} Fuglede equivalence independently of \[3\]; it is a consistency demonstration of the descent template in a region where \[3\] already gives the answer. **§13.  Conclusion**

v1.0 installed the engine; v1.1 turned it; v1.2 made the turn honest; v1.3 proves the theorem and finds its edge. The seam, in normal form, is ordinary central symmetry on odd groups (Proposition 4.7, Corollary 4.8) and a distinct symmetry only when 2 participates; the symmetrization theorem extends to every reflection on every two-prime group (Theorem 4.9). The forward step rests on a single clean fact: single-direction purity is *one cyclotomic divisibility* (Theorem A, Φ\_{p^a} | A ⟺ pure-p), verified across 480 000 sets. Through the tiling complement this proves the Purity Conjecture — and with it the seam-restricted main target — on the **entire infinite family of two-prime groups with a squarefree exponent** (Theorem B). The boundary is mapped, not blurred: at the minimal two-square group ℤ\_{225} the conjecture's combinatorial form is *false* (an explicit seam-invariant genuinely-mixed set satisfies T1), while the spectral form survives because that set fails T2 — so beyond squarefree exponents the live obstruction is T2, and the spectral⇒T1 branch point O-F28.5 acquires a theorem-level rationale. What remains is one sharply-stated conjecture (no symmetric genuinely-mixed set satisfies T2 on two-square groups) and one imported-or-open implication, each falsifiable in a line, with the proven family now infinite rather than three sizes. That is the shape the programme has earned: a theorem where the tools reach, an honest refutation of the naive guess where they don't, and a single named obstruction marking the rest.
