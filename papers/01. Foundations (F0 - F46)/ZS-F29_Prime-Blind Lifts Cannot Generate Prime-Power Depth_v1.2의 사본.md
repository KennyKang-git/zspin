**§4.  The Socle Obstruction**

**§4.1  Lemma 4.1 (Squarefree Span Lemma) and its socle reading.  \[PROVEN\]**

ℤ-span{ 1/p : p prime }  \=  { a/N ∈ ℚ (lowest terms) : N squarefree } .

Proof. (⊆) A finite sum Σ a\_p/p has common denominator Π p, squarefree; divisors of squarefree integers are squarefree. (⊇) Integers lie in the span; for N \= p₁⋯p\_r squarefree, Bézout gives x q′ \+ y p₁ \= 1 with q′ \= p₂⋯p\_r, whence 1/N \= x/p₁ \+ y/q′, and induction closes. □ (Checks G-13, G-14.)

**Lemma 4.1′ (socle reading).** \[PROVEN\]  In ℚ/ℤ, the subgroup generated by {1/p mod 1 : p prime} is exactly the socle — the set of elements of squarefree order — and the socle decomposes as ⊕\_p ℤ/p \[8\]. An element 1/p^k mod 1 has order p^k; for k ≥ 2 it lies outside the socle. (Check G-23.) The socle is precisely the multiplicative closure of the corpus transfer family computed in ZS-F27 Theorem 4.2, with Pontryagin dual ∏\_p 𝔽\_p: the present lemma identifies that closure intrinsically, as the largest subgroup of ℚ/ℤ reachable by prime-blind generation.

**§4.4  Theorem 4.6 (Universal Prime-Blind No-Go; group-extension form).  \[PROVEN; new in v1.1\]**

Let G be any group and π : G → ℚ/ℤ any homomorphism. π(G)  ⊆  socle(ℚ/ℤ)  \=  ⊕\_p ℤ/p ,

Proof. The image of a homomorphism is the subgroup generated by the images of the generators; all generator images lie in the socle, which is a subgroup (Lemma 4.1′); elements of order p² are outside it. □
