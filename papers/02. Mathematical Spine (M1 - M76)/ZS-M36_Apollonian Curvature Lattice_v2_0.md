(IMPORTED-1) Descartes Circle Theorem (Descartes 1643, modernized in Soddy 1936). For four mutually tangent circles with curvatures k₁, k₂, k₃, k₄ (with sign convention: positive for internally tangent, negative for the bounding circle), the curvatures satisfy:

*(k₁ \+ k₂ \+ k₃ \+ k₄)² \= 2(k₁² \+ k₂² \+ k₃² \+ k₄²)*

Solving for k₄ given k₁, k₂, k₃ yields two solutions, and Vieta's formula gives k₄' \= 2(k₁ \+ k₂ \+ k₃) − k₄, the Vieta-jumping operator that generates the gasket from the root quadruple.

*A \= ⟨S₀, S₁, S₂, S₃ | S\_i² \= e for i \= 0, 1, 2, 3⟩ ≅ (Z/2)\*4 (free product)*
